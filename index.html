      Help

      How do I submit a problem?

      Open Submit a problem, add a title, pick a category and describe what is going wrong.

      How do I find a problem?

      Use the search bar at the top, or tap Explore on the Problems page and choose a category.

      How is the Top 10 decided?

      The list is ordered by previously recorded votes.

      Where is my data saved?

      Submitted problems and votes are stored in this browser only. Other visitors do not see them, and clearing site data removes them.

      Can’t find your problem?

      Submit it so it appears in the list.
