        Help

        How do I use Workshop?
        1. Open a problem and select Take this problem to Workshop. You can also scroll to Workshop and choose a category and problem there.
        2. Write who you want to talk to and what you want to learn. Select Suggest answers if you want a draft to work from.
        3. Edit the draft so your questions and first test fit the problem. For late supplier payments, ask what happened the last time a buyer paid late and which bills were due while the supplier waited.
        4. Select Copy my plan and paste it into your notes.
        How do I verify a Top 12 problem?
        1. Open a paper under Top 12 Problems and read the description and evidence.
        2. Select the highlighted Verify with link to open the official publication.
        3. Check when the data was collected, who it covers, and what it measures.
        4. Use Workshop to plan a first test for any part of the question that needs more evidence.
        How do I find a problem in Explore?
        1. Select Explore to see a selection of problems you can test with sample data. Selecting Explore again draws a new set.
        2. Choose a category when you want to focus on one topic.
        3. Select a problem title to read the details and decide whether to take it to Workshop.
        Where is AI used?
        1. AI helps draft problem explanations and suggested Workshop answers.
        2. Check factual claims against the official source when one is linked.
        3. Edit Workshop suggestions to fit your situation and check your assumptions with people who have faced the problem.
        How do I choose a first test?
        1. Use Can test with sample data to find problems you can explore with made-up examples or a small demo.
        2. Use Talk to someone first when you need to understand what happens in practice. Ask someone to walk you through the last time they faced the problem.
        3. Open the problem in Workshop and write down one thing you want to learn from the test.
        How do I use Copy my plan?
        1. Fill in every Workshop answer, then select Copy my plan. It copies the category, the selected problem, and each Workshop question with its answer.
        2. If the app displays your selected plan in a text box, press Ctrl+C on Windows or Command+C on Mac. On a phone, use Copy in the text selection menu.
        3. When you see Plan copied or have copied it manually, paste it into your notes or a document.
